The workshop itself never had a story the way Livingston did. Four days, and it rained through most of them, sometimes barely, sometimes hard enough to chase us into doorways. Every day ran roughly the same shape: coffee, walk, more coffee, more walking, lunch, walking again until the light or our legs gave out. Turned out the rain was doing us a favor. Bozeman kept coming out the other side of every downpour looking better than it had any right to.

![The Sapphire Motel's red and white Oldsmobile parked out front](./01-oldsmobile.jpg)
*The Sapphire's red and white Oldsmobile, parked out front most mornings like it never left. Leica M10-R, Summilux-M 50mm f/1.4 Pre-ASPH, f/4.8.*

The first morning we met the rest of the group at Beacon Coffee Roasters. I liked the hat one of the baristas was wearing, so on the way out I asked to take her picture.

![A barista at Beacon Coffee Roasters, Bozeman](./02-barista.jpg)
*One of the baristas at Beacon Coffee Roasters, the hat worth stopping for. Leica M10-R, Summilux-M 50mm f/1.4 Pre-ASPH, f/1.4.*

We walked from there into the rain, and a few blocks on passed a man painting a house along our route. Six people with Leicas stopped and stared, and instead of asking us to move along he waved us over.

![A house painter encountered on a walk through Bozeman](./03-house-painter.jpg)
*A house painter who didn't mind an audience. Leica M10-R, Summilux-M 50mm f/1.4 Pre-ASPH, f/5.6.*

The house next to his had come out of the last downpour looking freshly repainted, colors a shade more saturated than they had any business being.

![A house in Bozeman with vivid colors after rain](./04-house-colors.jpg)
*One of the houses we passed, minutes after the rain let up. Leica M10-R, Summilux-M 50mm f/1.4 Pre-ASPH, f/3.4.*

That afternoon was the one real photo session of the week, portraits on the covered second-floor walkway outside the dining area at the Rendezvous Dining Pavilion, Montana State's dining hall. Thorsten sat Brandon inside by the window and had the rest of us shoot from outside, hunting the glass for reflections. Brandon's a sophomore in high school and wants to direct movies eventually. He held still longer than anyone else did all week.

![Brandon, a workshop participant, photographed through a dining hall window](./05-brandon.jpg)
*Brandon, shot through the dining hall glass for the reflection, Rendezvous Dining Pavilion. Leica M10-R, Summilux-M 50mm f/1.4 Pre-ASPH, f/1.4.*

Back at the Sapphire that evening, Thorsten sat down at the old typewriter the hotel keeps in the lobby like it's still 1962. I caught him in silhouette before he noticed.

![Thorsten silhouetted at an old typewriter in the Sapphire Motel lobby](./06-typewriter.jpg)
*Thorsten at the Sapphire's lobby typewriter, another prop from 1962. Leica M10-R, Summilux-M 50mm f/1.4 Pre-ASPH, f/4.0.*

The rest of the week blurred into itself a little, the way workshop days do. We ducked into a hat shop the next afternoon, mostly to get out of the rain, and Ellen, another one of the group, tried on half the store while we waited it out.

![Ellen, a workshop participant, trying on hats in a Bozeman shop](./07-ellen-hats.jpg)
*Ellen, working her way through the hat shop's stock. Leica M10-R, Summilux-M 50mm f/1.4 Pre-ASPH, f/1.4.*

A day later Thorsten fell for a house on our route. Clean and simple, he kept saying, architecturally speaking, and asked me to photograph him in front of it. He'd been finding me subjects all week and hadn't asked for one of his own until then.

![Thorsten standing in front of a house he loved in Bozeman](./08-thorsten-house.jpg)
*Thorsten, in front of the house he loved for how clean and simple it was. Leica M10-R, Summilux-M 50mm f/1.4 Pre-ASPH, f/3.4.*

Even the fire hydrants got the treatment that week. Red against wet green grass isn't subtle, but subtlety wasn't really the assignment.

![A red fire hydrant against wet green grass in Bozeman](./09-hydrant.jpg)
*Red and green, no subtlety required. Leica M10-R, Summilux-M 50mm f/1.4 Pre-ASPH, f/5.6.*

The workshop wrapped and I still didn't have the hat. I went looking anyway, Boot Barn this time, walls of boots and not much in black felt worth the drive. I ended up ordering one online once I got home, which I'm fairly sure Thorsten would call cheating.

![Rows of boots inside Boot Barn in Bozeman](./10-boot-barn.jpg)
*Boot Barn, still hunting the black hat. Leica M10-R, Summilux-M 50mm f/1.4 Pre-ASPH, f/1.4.*

Then the airport, waiting on the flight home, four days of rain finally behind the plane instead of over it.

![A plane on the runway at Bozeman Yellowstone International Airport](./11-airport.jpg)
*Waiting on the flight out of Bozeman. Leica M10-R, Summilux-M 50mm f/1.4 Pre-ASPH, f/16.*

Every bit of it was better in the rain than it would have been in sun: the streets, the houses, even the wait outside a boot shop for a hat I'd end up buying from my couch a week later.
